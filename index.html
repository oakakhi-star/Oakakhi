Your Host

Born on these ridges. Returned to build.

Nishant grew up watching these peaks from this very hillside. He left to learn, to explore, and to work with global hospitality companies, yet the mountains never quite let him go.

He came back not to visit, but to build. Oakakhi is the result: a home where strangers become guests, guests become friends, and the Himalaya forms the backdrop to honest conversation.

He will walk you through the forest at dawn and name each birdsong. On winter nights he will point to Orion rising over the ridge and trace the constellations one by one. Oakakhi is his love letter to Ranikhet, and every guest is invited to read it.

Arrival

The journey to Oakakhi

The final stretch is walked through forest, by design. The path itself is the first experience: a gentle crossing from one world into another.

  • AddressVillage Dadgaliya, PO Kalika, Ranikhet, Uttarakhand 263652
  • By roadDelhi to Ranikhet via NH 9, about 320 km. Taxis are available from Kathgodam and Ramnagar.
  • By railKathgodam (KGM) is the nearest station, about 85 km away, with good connections from Delhi and Lucknow.
  • By airPantnagar Airport is about 120 km away, followed by a scenic mountain drive.
  • On arrivalParking is about 200 metres from the house. Leave the car there and follow a short forest trail of around 200 metres to Oakakhi. Nishant will guide you in.

Night Experience

Stargazing at Oakakhi

At six thousand feet, far from any city's glow, Oakakhi sits beneath one of the clearest night skies in Kumaon. When the clouds part, the Milky Way arches from ridge to ridge.

Nishant has spent years watching these skies. He will show you Orion rising over the eastern ridge in winter, find the Pleiades, trace the path of Jupiter and share why the ancients named the stars as they did. No telescope is needed. Only clear air, warm blankets and time.

Best season

October to March for the clearest skies. Winter nights are cold, and extraordinary.

The session

An hour and a half to two hours with Nishant: constellations, planets and the stories of the Kumaoni night.

Bring

Warm layers and curiosity. Hot chai and blankets are on us. The sky provides the rest.

Some nights the sky feels like a ceiling you forgot was always there.

Dawn Session

Bird watching in the oak forest

The forest around Oakakhi is a living library of birdsong. The Khalij pheasant, Himalayan bulbul, blue whistling thrush and dozens more make their home here through the year.

Nishant knows their songs before he sees them; he was raised listening to these woods. A dawn session with him is less a guided tour and more an introduction to neighbours you never knew you had.

Best time

First light, between 5:30 and 8:00 in the morning, when the forest is most alive.

Often seen

Khalij pheasant, Himalayan bulbul, blue whistling thrush, red billed blue magpie, and seasonal warblers and flycatchers.

Duration

Two to three slow hours through the oak grove. Less a hike, more a listening session.

Before the mountains reveal themselves, the birds announce them.

Daily

Forest walks through the oaks

The oaks around Oakakhi are old, their roots deep in Himalayan soil and their canopy a ceiling of green that shifts with every breeze.

Walking them with Nishant is different from walking alone. He will show you which mushrooms rise after the monsoon, which bark smells of rain and where the deer come to drink. The walk is not about distance covered. It is about the world noticed.

The route

A circular walk through the grove around Oakakhi, two to four kilometres at your pace. Suited to every level of fitness.

What you learn

Local ecology, Kumaoni forest traditions and medicinal plants: knowledge that lives in people, not in books.

When

Morning or late afternoon. Autumn brings golden light; the monsoon turns everything a vivid green.

The forest does not tell you what to think. It simply gives you room to think it.

Culture

A village walk through Dadgaliya

Dadgaliya is not a tourist attraction. It is a living Kumaoni village, and Nishant grew up here. Walk it with him and you are not a visitor on a tour; you are a guest being introduced to a community.

See how Kumaoni homes are built, how the terraces are worked and how grain is still ground the old way. You may share tea in a courtyard, or be invited to watch a family cook. Such moments cannot be scheduled, but Nishant creates the conditions for them.

Duration

Two to three hours, a slow and unscripted walk through the village and its farmland.

Expect

Traditional stone architecture, terraced fields, village temples and genuine conversation.

A gentle note

Please dress respectfully and ask before photographing people. Nishant will guide you.

The best way to understand a place is to walk it with someone who belongs to it.

Evening

Bonfire evenings under an open sky

There is something about fire that makes people honest. After dinner the mountains go dark, the stars come out, and around the warmth and the crackle, conversations happen that guests remember for years.

Some evenings Nishant plays music; some evenings he simply listens. Guests sometimes bring instruments, and sometimes no one speaks at all. The fire asks for no agenda, only that you sit with it a while.

When

Most evenings, weather permitting, and most beautiful in winter when the air is sharp and the peaks are white.

Included

The fire, chai or local herbal tea, blankets and Nishant's company. Sometimes music, always conversation.

The mood

Informal and unhurried. Some fires last two hours, some until midnight. You decide.

Strangers met around that fire. They left as something more.

Guided

Ranikhet, as a local knows it

Ranikhet holds history, temples and views that most visitors only skim. With Nishant, you go deeper.

He knows which corner of Chaubatia catches the best morning light, when Kalika Temple is at its quietest, and which road offers the clearest line to Nanda Devi. Transport is arranged, and the pace is entirely yours.

Places

Kalika Temple, the Chaubatia orchards, Ranikhet Golf Course, Jhula Devi Temple, Bhalu Dam and the ridge viewpoints.

Transport

A comfortable local taxi arranged by Nishant, who accompanies you with stories at every stop.

Duration

A half day of four hours, or a full day shaped around what you wish to see.

Every town has a version for visitors and a version for those who belong. Nishant shows you the second.

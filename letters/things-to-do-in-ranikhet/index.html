Things to do in Ranikhet, the slow way

Temples, orchards, an old golf course in the pines and the ridge views most visitors drive past. A local's guide to Ranikhet.

Ranikhet is a quiet hill town in the Kumaon Himalaya of Uttarakhand, set among pine and oak at around six thousand feet. It is the kind of place that rewards an unhurried pace. Here is how we suggest spending your days, from the ridge where Oakakhi sits.

Kalika Temple and the forest around it

Oakakhi is a short walk from the ancient Kalika Temple. Go early in the morning, when the bells are the only sound and the light falls through the deodar and oak. The path back to the house is one of our favourite short walks in all of Ranikhet.

Ranikhet Golf Course

Laid out on an open meadow ringed by pines, the old golf course near Kalika is often described as one of the highest in Asia. Even if you never pick up a club, the walk along its edge on a clear day, with the snow peaks in the distance, is worth the visit.

Chaubatia Gardens and Bhalu Dam

Chaubatia is known for its orchards of apple, apricot and plum, and for wide views of the Himalaya. A little beyond lies Bhalu Dam, a small, still lake in the forest that makes a peaceful place for a picnic.

Jhula Devi Temple

Close to Chaubatia, this small temple is famous for the thousands of bells tied by devotees whose wishes have been granted. It is a moving place, and a lovely stop on the way back from the orchards.

The Kumaon Regimental Centre Museum

Ranikhet is a cantonment town with a long military history. The regimental museum tells the story of the Kumaon and Naga regiments and makes a thoughtful hour on a rainy afternoon.

Village walks and Kumaoni food

Some of the best moments are not in any guidebook. Walk through Dadgaliya village with Nishant, see the terraced fields and stone homes, and share tea in a courtyard. Back at Oakakhi, the evening meal is home style Kumaoni food.

Stargazing and bonfire evenings

Far from city lights, Ranikhet has wonderfully dark skies, especially from October to March. End the day around the bonfire, then step away from the fire to find Orion rising over the ridge.

Planning your days. Nishant arranges a guided half day or full day around Ranikhet, with a comfortable local taxi and stories at every stop. Simply ask when you book.

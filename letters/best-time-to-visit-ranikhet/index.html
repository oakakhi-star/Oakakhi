The best time to visit Ranikhet, season by season

Clear winter skies, spring birdsong, green monsoon mist and golden autumn. What each season brings to the Kumaon hills.

Every season changes the ridge around Oakakhi completely. There is no wrong time to come; there is only the time that suits what you are looking for.

October to November: the clearest views

After the monsoon, the air is washed clean and the Himalaya stand out sharply on the horizon. Days are bright and comfortable, evenings are cool, and the night skies begin to clear. For many, this is the finest time of year.

December to February: winter light

Cold, crisp and quiet. The snow peaks glow at sunrise and dusk, bonfires feel especially welcome, and the stars are extraordinary. Pack warm layers: nights in the hills are properly cold.

March to May: spring and birdsong

The forest wakes. Rhododendrons flower on the hillsides, birds are in full song and the days are warm and pleasant, a welcome escape from the heat of the plains.

June to September: monsoon mist

Rain and mist move through the oak forest, and everything turns a deep, vivid green. Views of the peaks are rarer, but for those who love the sound of rain on a timber roof, the monsoon is magical.

Questions we are often asked

When can you see the Himalaya from Ranikhet?

The clearest views of the snow peaks usually come from October to March, after the monsoon has washed the air clean.

Is Ranikhet good in the monsoon?

Yes, for those who love mist and green forests. Views of the peaks are rarer, but the oak forest is at its most beautiful.

What is the best season for stargazing in Ranikhet?

October to March offers the clearest night skies.

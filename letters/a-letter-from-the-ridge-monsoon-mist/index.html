A letter from the ridge: when the mist arrives

Some mornings the valley disappears, and the house floats in a white silence. A short letter from Oakakhi in the monsoon.

Some mornings, the valley simply disappears.

The mist rises from below the ridge before dawn and settles among the oaks. The house floats in it. You hear the forest more than you see it: water dripping from the leaves, a whistling thrush somewhere close, the soft drum of rain on the roof.

On days like these there is nowhere to go and nothing to do, and that is exactly the point. Tea tastes better. Books get finished. Conversations run long.

Then, often in the afternoon, the white parts like a curtain, and for a few minutes the whole valley returns, rinsed and glittering and impossibly green.

If you have never seen the hills in the monsoon, come and watch the mist arrive. We will keep the chai warm.

With warmth, from the Kalika ridge.

How to reach Ranikhet and Oakakhi

By road from Delhi, by train to Kathgodam, or by air to Pantnagar. Everything you need for the journey, including the short forest walk at the end.

Oakakhi sits in Village Dadgaliya, near Kalika, on the ridges of Ranikhet in Uttarakhand. The journey is part of the pleasure: the road climbs from the plains into pine forest, and the final stretch is walked through the trees.

By road

From Delhi, Ranikhet is about 320 km by road via NH 9. Most guests drive or take a taxi, and the mountain section of the journey is scenic. Taxis are available from Kathgodam and Ramnagar.

By train

Kathgodam (KGM) is the nearest railway station, about 85 km from Oakakhi, with good overnight and day connections from Delhi and Lucknow. From Kathgodam, a taxi brings you up into the hills.

By air

Pantnagar Airport is the nearest airport, about 120 km away, followed by a scenic mountain drive.

Parking and the last 200 metres

Parking is about 200 metres from the house. Leave the car there and follow a short forest trail of around 200 metres to Oakakhi. It is an easy walk, and Nishant will meet you and guide you in. Please pack so that your luggage is comfortable to carry a short distance.

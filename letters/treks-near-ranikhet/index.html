Treks near Ranikhet, from forest walks to glaciers

Pandukholi, Bharatkot, the Mahavtar forest and further, to the Pindari Glacier. Treks of every length that begin from the Kumaon ridges.

Ranikhet makes an ideal base for walking in the Kumaon Himalaya. Within an hour or two there are short forest trails and summit viewpoints, and a little further lie some of the most beautiful multi day treks in Uttarakhand.

Forest walks from Oakakhi

The gentlest trek begins at the door. A circular walk of two to four kilometres through the oak grove around Oakakhi suits every level of fitness, and Nishant will show you the trees, the medicinal plants and where the deer come to drink.

Pandukholi

A forest trail climbs to the high meadow of Pandukholi, linked by legend to the Pandavas. The sunrise and sunset views over the Himalaya from the top are unforgettable, and the walk through the forest is beautiful in every season.

Bharatkot

A rewarding day walk to a ridge top with wide views of the snow peaks, including Trishul. A good choice for walkers who want a summit without a long journey.

The Mahavtar bamboo forest

A quiet walk through bamboo and pine, valued as much for its stillness as for the trail itself.

Multi day treks: Pindari Glacier and Khalia Top

For those who wish to go further, the Pindari Glacier trek follows river valleys and villages to the foot of the glacier over several days. Khalia Top, near Munsiyari, offers a shorter high camp with remarkable views of the Panchachuli peaks.

Trekking with us. Longer treks are curated by Turiya Trails, our partner in mountain journeys, with experienced local trek leaders. Nishant, himself a trek leader and mountaineer, will help you choose and arrange the right trek for your stay.
